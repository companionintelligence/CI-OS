# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion OS's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion OS source in this repository for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion OS uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).

Ubuntu and other upstream packages keep their own licenses. For third-party
assets bundled in compiled/binary form — where the upstream license isn't
visible from the file itself — see [`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion OS user?

If you use Companion OS for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion OS?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute this repository under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion OS?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion OS?

No. Selling Companion OS, including shipping it on hardware or hosting it as a service, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to Companion Portal?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Companion Hub, Companion Memory, Companion Capture, Companion Marketplace, and the Companion Browser Extension use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories.
